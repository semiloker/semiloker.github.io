Home.

        

We walk through time as if it were a corridor,
walls lined with memories, doors opening to choices.
Each step is both freedom and necessity —
freedom, because we choose;
necessity, because we cannot choose not to walk.

The world is not a puzzle to be solved,
but a mirror that reflects the questions we carry.
Meaning is not hidden in the stars,
it is woven into the act of looking at them.

To live is to interpret,
to interpret is to create,
and to create is to leave behind
a trace of ourselves in the infinite silence.
