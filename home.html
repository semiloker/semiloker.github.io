Home.

I’m someone who likes to understand how things actually work.
Not just on the surface, but underneath — where signals move,
logic forms, and small decisions quietly shape bigger systems.
Curiosity is the constant driver here: the kind that doesn’t ask
“what does it do?” but “why does it behave this way?”
I work at the intersection of hardware and software,
where ideas become physical and mistakes become lessons.
Microcontrollers, electronics, low-level code — these aren’t just tools,
they’re ways of thinking.
I enjoy building things step by step, breaking them, fixing them,
and learning from the silence when something doesn’t respond as expected.
This site is a reflection of that process.
Not a finished product, but a living space for experiments, notes, and progress.
I don’t chase perfection — I chase understanding.
Everything here exists for one simple reason: to move forward, one iteration at a time.
